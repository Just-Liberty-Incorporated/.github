## Our Mission: The Rule of Law for Everyone

The American legal system is procedurally complex, expensive, and structurally tilted against ordinary people. Most legal technology treats the law as a text-generation problem—producing generic AI essays that hallucinate citations while leaving self-represented litigants to drown in procedural traps, missed deadlines, and unverified records.

**Just Liberty Incorporated treats the law as a deterministic systems-engineering, record-verification, and procedural-routing problem.**

We do not practice law, give legal advice, or represent parties in court. Instead, we build deterministic offline formatting engines, court rules compilers, and public-records verification tools that give self-represented litigants, tenants, consumers, families, and citizen whistleblowers the procedural precision, verified structure, and dignity of an institutional law firm.

## 🏛️ Expanded Whistleblower, Fraud & Administrative Accountability Architecture

While version 0.1.0 was forged to triage **Street-Level Storefront Fraud** (ghost clinics, dark windows during posted hours, and inactive NPIs billing Medicare/Medicaid under Executive Order 14395), JustLegal OS now encompasses the complete spectrum of **Systemic Institutional Mismanagement & Government Contractor Fraud**.

When ordinary citizens experience systemic fallout—such as automated unemployment integrity freezes, non-responsive state agencies, and multi-billion-dollar contractor IT failures—traditional advice often defaults to defeatist brush-offs like *"go vote,"* *"call 211,"* or *"post on social media."* JustLegal OS replaces platitudes with **deterministic systems-engineering and statutory bypass routing**:

* **Navigating Sovereign Immunity (*Stevens* Boundary & Valid Bypasses):** Under *Vermont Agency of Natural Resources v. United States ex rel. Stevens*, 529 U.S. 765 (2000), states cannot be sued in qui tam lawsuits. The engine evaluates 5 lawful bypasses: *Ex parte Young* (prospective injunctive relief against officials), Title II ADA abrogation (*United States v. Georgia*, 546 U.S. 151 (2006)), § 504 Rehabilitation Act Spending Clause waivers, and private prime contractor liability (31 U.S.C. § 3729(b)(2)(A)(ii)).
* **FRCP Rule 20(a)(1) Multi-Plaintiff Joinder:** Because pro se litigants cannot represent a Rule 23 class (*Oxendine v. Williams*), the engine formats joint actions for 2–25 affected filers on a single docket, splitting one $405 filing fee and curing mootness.
* **Pre-Filing Open Records Suite (CORA & FOIA):** Generates formal records requests under state and federal sunshine laws (Colorado CORA C.R.S. § 24-72-201, FOIA 5 U.S.C. § 552) with 501(c)(3) public interest fee waivers, tracking compliance windows with court holiday tolling.
* **Vernacular-to-Doctrine Engine:** Translates colloquial grievances ("they committed treason", "they took kickbacks") into actionable statutory claims (18 U.S.C. §§ 666/1346, 42 U.S.C. § 1983, Civil RICO 18 U.S.C. § 1964(c)) paired with element-by-element evidence blueprints.

## Statutory Whistleblower Verification: DOJ WHIP & Sealed Qui Tam ([justlegal.me/qui-tam](https://justlegal.me/qui-tam))

Under the **Department of Justice Fraud Whistleblower Incentive Program (WHIP on america.gov/fraud & fraud.gov)**, **DOJ Directive 26-12**, and **Executive Order 14395**, federal fraud enforcement operates on a coordinated two-tier track:

Just Liberty Incorporated provides a **free, public-interest pre-seal verification pipeline** that turns citizen observations and insider disclosures into cryptographically hashed, Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 9(b)-grounded dossiers:

* **Track 1: Direct Citizen Whistleblower Reporting (WHIP via america.gov/fraud):** Built for street-level observations (ghost daycares, shuttered clinics, strip-mall billing mills) and verified independent analysis under 28 U.S.C. § 530C(b)(1)(L). Automatically generates character-limited ≤ 1,950 char submissions, scrubs PII, and compiles an attached, tamper-proof SHA-256 photographic PDF packet (`WHIP_Evidence_Packet.pdf`).
* **Track 2: Sealed False Claims Act Qui Tam Lawsuits (31 U.S.C. §§ 3729–3733):** For large contractor and institutional healthcare schemes. Evaluates statutory relator shares (15%–25% intervened; 25%–30% declined under 31 U.S.C. § 3730(d)). Strictly prepared for filing under seal by retained contingency relator counsel ($0 upfront).
* **Preventing Fatal Seal & Disclosure Violations:** Strictly educates whistleblowers on **31 U.S.C. § 3730(b)(2)** and the **Public Disclosure Bar (§ 3730(e)(4))** to stop them from publishing allegations on social media or submitting non-public insider records to public portals prematurely.
* **Deterministic 4-State Verification Receipt:** Enforces the principle that *"the valuable object is a refusal, not a grade."* Classifies evidence across *Who, What, When, Where, How, Scienter, and Materiality* strictly as `VERIFIED`, `USER_STATED`, `MISSING`, or `LOOKUP_FAILED` against live government registries (CMS NPPES, USAspending, SAM.gov), failing closed when required proof is absent.
* **Standardized Calling Card v1.1:** Outputs the recognized `[JUSTLEGAL STANDARD v1.1 | WHIP & EO-14395 PRE-VERIFIED DISCLOSURE]` diagnostic header block for 90-second triage by federal investigators, DOJ intake officers, and qui tam counsel.
* **5-Gate Pre-Submission Lock:** Zero autonomous filing. All submissions require Receipt Check, Lawfulness Check, Route Clearance, Linter Pass, and Explicit Final Human Review.
