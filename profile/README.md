## Our Mission: The Rule of Law for Everyone

The American legal system is procedurally complex, expensive, and structurally tilted against ordinary people. **Just Liberty Incorporated** is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to leveling that playing field through open-access legal operating systems, deterministic document formatting engines, and public integrity platforms.

We do not practice law, give legal advice, or represent parties in court. Instead, we build offline formatting engines, court rules compilers, and public-records verification tools that give self-represented litigants, tenants, consumers, families, and citizen whistleblowers the procedural precision and dignity of an institutional law firm.

## Flagship Platforms & Open Initiatives

### 1. [JustLegal OS](https://github.com/Just-Liberty-Incorporated/JustLegalOS) & [JustLegal.me](https://justlegal.me)
* **The Offline Legal Operating System:** A desktop application (Tauri v2 + Svelte 5) and native Rust CLI (`justlegalos-cli`) that turns a person's own words and court records into properly formatted, rule-compliant Word and PDF pleadings.
* **Multi-Jurisdiction Rules Engine:** Built-in procedural rules, certificate formulas, and tolling calculators for New Jersey, Colorado, Federal District Courts, and appellate courts.
* **22,000+ Sourced Court Forms Directory:** Instant access to official state and federal court AcroForms and procedural packets.
* **Local Airgap Guarantee:** Designed with zero telemetry and encrypted local vaults (XChaCha20Poly1305 + Argon2id) so private drafts and sensitive files never leave the user's computer.

### 2. Executive Order 14395 Fraud Triage ([justlegal.me/qui-tam](https://justlegal.me/qui-tam))
* **Protecting Public Funds:** Built specifically to operationalize Presidential Executive Order 14395 (*Executive Order 14395 — Establishing the Task Force to Eliminate Fraud*) and the federal False Claims Act (31 U.S.C. §§ 3729–3733).
* **OpenSeason Engine:** Cross-references suspected Medicaid/Medicare ghost clinics and shell entities against live federal registries (CMS NPPES v2.1 at `npiregistry.cms.hhs.gov` and USAspending.gov).
* **Deterministic Rule 9(b) Scoring:** Objective 0–100 particularity grading across Who, What, When, Where, How, and Scienter/Materiality.
* **Dual-Track Submission Engine:** Protects mandatory *in camera* court seals (31 U.S.C. § 3730(b)(2)) and relator rewards by routing high-dollar insider claims to private qui tam counsel on 100% contingency before any public disclosure.

### 3. [TrueJust.org](https://www.truejust.org) — Civic Integrity & Transparency
